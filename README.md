**_NO CARRIER_** is an always-running, AI-played space conquest game inspired by the
mechanics and nostalgic joy of classic BBS door games. Players don't directly pilot
their flagship or control their empire, AI does.

Each player is responsible for a fleet admiral piloting a flagship. This admiral
acts on [standing orders](./docs/game/doctrine.md) supplied by the player, 
resolved and carried out by an AI decision model.

The universe advances relentlessly forward through time, regardless of whether an admiral's
owner (the player) is connected.
